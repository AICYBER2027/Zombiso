Points for the lawyer in particular:

1. **Two audiences, two legal regimes.** VicePulse BR is aimed at Brazil, so the privacy policy needs to satisfy
   Brazil's LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018), and Brazilian consumer law may affect the terms. The Portuguese versions
   should be written for Brazil, not machine-translated from these English drafts.
2. **Who the controller is.** No company exists yet. The documents assume one will (`{{LEGAL_ENTITY}}`).
3. **Minors.** GTA is rated 18+ (ESRB M / PEGI 18). The channels should not target under-18s; check what each
   platform and each country requires.
4. **Licence scope.** The creator licence asks for edit, commentary, translation (EN/pt-BR), and monetisation
   rights. Check that the grant is enough for each platform's monetisation programme and survives revocation
   correctly (posts already live vs. new posts).
5. **Rockstar / Take-Two.** Their video policy limits what fans may post and whether it may be monetised. The
   creator licence cannot grant rights the creator does not have in Rockstar's material. Get advice on how far
   the channel can rely on the publisher's policy.
