## 2. What we collect

| Who | Data | Why | Legal basis (GDPR / LGPD) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creators who license footage to us | Name, contact email, channel name and link, signed licence, payment details if paid, footage you upload | To run the licence: use, credit and pay you, and handle revocations | Contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b); LGPD art. 7 V) |

| Our own platform accounts | Access tokens for our own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts; statistics about our own posts (views, likes, comments counts, watch time) | To publish our videos and see how they perform | Legitimate interest |

We do **not** collect data about the people who watch our videos beyond the aggregated statistics the platforms
give channel owners. We do not sell personal data. We do not use it for advertising profiles.

### Data from Google / YouTube

When we connect our own YouTube channels, our software uses YouTube API Services. Our use of information
received from Google APIs adheres to the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy
(https://policies.google.com/privacy). We use that access only to upload videos to our own channels, delete them,
and read their statistics. You can revoke our access to a Google account at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

### Data from TikTok and Meta (Instagram)

We use TikTok's Content Posting API and the Instagram API only for our own accounts, to publish our videos and
read their statistics. We follow TikTok's and Meta's developer terms and policies.
